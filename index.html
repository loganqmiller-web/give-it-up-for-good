05 / THE YEAR-ROUND IDEA

Make giving a campus habit.

Our recommendation: start communication on move-in day, reinforce it all year, then test expanded collection where university partners can manage it responsibly.

✺
AUG–SEP · FALL

Start at move-in

Put a simple donation message in welcome materials, on residence hall flyers, and in RA conversations. Link everything to one QR-friendly guide.

Students learn about it early ↗
❄
OCT–FEB · YEAR-ROUND

Keep it visible

Share small reminders through residence hall screens, social posts, and a midyear closet clean-out challenge. Feature existing year-round resources.

Donations don't pile up ↗
✿
MAR–APR · SPRING

Prepare, don't panic

Publish the verified accepted-items list, drop-off dates, map or location details, and clear reminders before students start packing.

Less last-minute confusion ↗
↗
APR–MAY · MOVE-OUT

Give it up for good

Direct students to official spring bins, promote convenient handoffs, and track donations to learn where students still face barriers.

More items get another use ↗
WHAT IT TAKES

A realistic pilot, not just more posters.

Adding collection points requires real operations planning. Start small, learn, and scale only when demand and partners support it.

01
Partners & approval

Work with University Housing, sustainability staff, facilities, and receiving nonprofits.

02
Space & service

Confirm secure bins, accessibility, item restrictions, collection frequency, and storage.

03
Measurement

Track QR guide visits, student awareness, collected pounds, contamination, and staffing demands.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

The good-to-know stuff.

These answers distinguish existing USC services from what our student team is proposing.

Is there already a year-round Give It Up for Good collection station?+

We have not verified a permanent year-round station operated under the Give It Up for Good name. USC's separate Gamecock CommUnity Shop currently accepts donations year-round, and our project proposes exploring additional approved collection points with Housing and sustainability staff.

Can off-campus students participate?+

Give It Up for Good's spring collection is aimed primarily at residence hall students. USC recommends that off-campus students explore local organizations and reuse options; the Gamecock CommUnity Shop also accepts appropriate items. Check each organization's rules first.

Can I leave a mini-fridge at the CommUnity Shop?+

Do not assume so. USC has described small appliances as eligible in spring Give It Up for Good collections, but that does not establish that the CommUnity Shop accepts them. Contact the intended recipient before dropping one off.

When will spring 2027 collection bins open?+

This website does not have verified 2027 dates. USC's 2025 overview described boxes appearing around two weeks before move-out. Check the newest University Housing announcements for the exact spring collection schedule.

Is this an official university website?+

No. This is a student-created campaign concept by Team 7, prepared in November 2026. It links to official USC resources for existing service information, and marks proposed features as proposals.
